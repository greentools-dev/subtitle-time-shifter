Frequently Asked Questions

How do I determine the exact delay required?

Play your video in VLC or QuickTime. Note the timestamp when a specific character speaks the first word, and compare it to the first timestamp in the original SRT file. The difference is your required offset.

What format do SRT timestamps use?

Standard SRT timestamps follow HH:MM:SS,mmm where hours, minutes, and seconds are separated by colons and milliseconds are separated by a comma.

Can this tool handle both SRT and WebVTT?

Yes. The parser recognizes both comma-delimited SRT and period-delimited WebVTT timestamp notations.

Does this tool work completely offline?

Yes. Once loaded, all calculations function offline without requiring an internet connection.
